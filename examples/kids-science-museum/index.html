Splash · water & floating

Why do some things sink?

Build a dam on the 12-metre water table, race boats through the locks and crank water uphill with an Archimedes screw.

Waterproof aprons provided

Spark · electricity & magnets

Where does electricity come from?

Light a bulb with your own two hands, make your hair stand on end and steer a ball bearing through the giant magnet maze.

Static dome: ages 7+

Sky · air, flight & space

How high can a paper rocket go?

Fire air rockets eight metres up, ride a hover-cushion across the floor and lie back under the star dome.

Star dome every 30 minutes

Grow · plants, bugs & bodies

What's alive in here?

Watch our leafcutter ants at work, race your own heartbeat and plant a seed in a pot to take home.

Seed pots included with every child ticket

Getting here

14 Greenhouse Lane, Riverside. It's a 5-minute walk from Riverside station, and buses 4 and 17 stop outside. Our car park is pay-and-display and has 12 family bays.

Everyone's welcome

The whole building is step-free, with lifts and a Changing Places toilet. You can borrow ear defenders and fidget kits at the desk. Quiet mornings run 9:30–11am on the first Sunday of every month.

Snacks & buggies

Café Sprout serves hot lunches and kids' lunchboxes. There's also a picnic room if you'd rather bring your own. Buggies can be left in the free, staffed buggy park.

Good to know

Questions grown-ups ask

Do we need to book?

You don't have to, but we do recommend it. Weekends and school holidays often sell out by mid-morning, and online tickets are guaranteed entry. If there's space on the day you can buy tickets at the door.

What age is it best for?

Everything is designed for ages 5 to 12. Under-5s are welcome and free, and they love the Splash zone and the Tiny Tinkerers sessions. A few exhibits have a minimum age, and it's marked on the sign.

Can children visit without an adult?

No. Every child needs to be with an adult aged 18 or over, and one adult can bring up to four children.

Will my children get wet?

Probably a bit! We lend waterproof aprons in Splash, and there are hand dryers at the zone exit. A spare pair of socks never hurts.

Can we leave and come back?

Yes. Ask for a hand stamp at the exit and you can come back in any time on the same day.

How does the annual family pass work?

For £85 a year, two named adults and up to four children get unlimited visits, early entry at 9am on Saturdays and 10% off in the café.
