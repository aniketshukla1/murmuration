How does Ledgerly work out what to set aside?

It looks at your income and deductible expenses so far this year and estimates your federal, state and self-employment tax. You can always adjust the rate yourself. It's a guide, not tax advice.

Can Ledgerly move money out of my bank?

No. Bank connections are read-only. Automatic transfers to a tax pot only happen between accounts you choose, and you can switch them off at any time.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Export everything as CSV or PDF first, then close your account and we delete your data.
