Practice

How a building comes to stand

We take on four or five buildings a year, so the people who draw yours are the people who raise it.

  1. 01

    Walk the site

    A day on your land before a single line: where the sun comes up, where the snow lies, which trees stay.

  2. 02

    Draw every joint

    Plans at 1:50, then every connection at 1:5. Nothing is left for the site to work out.

  3. 03

    Cut in the workshop

    Our carpenters cut the frame dry and to the millimetre, from spruce and larch grown within 80 km.

  4. 04

    Raise it

    The frame goes up bent by bent in about two days. When the ridge is on, we tie a small spruce to it and everyone eats: the Richtfest.

Work · Public buildings

Small public buildings a village can look after

Kindergartens, halls and shelters, built by local carpenters from local wood, with every part made to be replaced.

  • Kinderhaus Sonnenhalde

    Waltenhofen · 2022 · 3 groups

    One long roof over three rooms and a covered porch, so wet days still happen outside.

  • Dorfsaal

    Ofterschwang · 2020 · 180 seats

    A village hall whose frame is left bare inside, so everyone can see what holds the roof up.

  • Bergwacht station

    Oberjoch · 2018 · 14 panels

    A mountain rescue base made in our workshop and closed in on site in four days.

  • Bus shelters

    Oberallgäu · 2017 · 11 shelters

    One kit of larch parts for eleven villages, each shelter turned toward its own view.

Six in the evening, late October. The last batten is on and the lamps are lit.

Material

Wood is the one building material that grows back. We use it plainly: joints you can see, larch left to weather silver, nothing glued that could be pegged, so the next carpenter can repair a house instead of replacing it.
